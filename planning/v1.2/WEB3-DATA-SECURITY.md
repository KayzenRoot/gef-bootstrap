STATUS: CONDITIONAL HIGH-ASSURANCE RESEARCH; NOT INSTALLED, IMPLEMENTED, AUDITED OR AUTHORIZED FOR REAL ASSET MANAGEMENT. Extends WEB3-FACTORY-PROFILE.md, GEF approved Security/Proof Graph/Test Impact and proposed SaaS/Data Guardian. Codex alone implements future code/tests/CI. ## 1. Security model before selecting technology
Distinguish immutable public blockchain data, metadata visible from wallets/transactions/events/RPC, private offchain customer data, backend application service secrets, deployer/admin signing keys, noncustodial user wallets, and any custodial assets or escrow. A single “encrypted Web3” switch cannot protect all these planes. Onchain user PII, passport documents, authentication secrets, card information and personal profiles should not be published, including identifying hashes that permit dictionary or correlation attacks. Keep sensitive state offchain and use only scope-approved commitments/proofs with analysis of deanonymization and irrevocable publication risk. Data classification dictates encrypted storage, masking, logging and deletion/retention.

## 2. First-party future mechanisms
DATA-W3-01 Offchain Confidential Vault: encrypt sensitive PII/business records using authenticated encryption and envelope data keys, tenant-specific or project-appropriate key domains, KMS/HSM-backed key-encryption keys, per-service least privilege, automated rotation/revocation rehearsal, disaster/backup restoration and crypto-erasure limits. AWS KMS envelope design is one candidate; equivalent local/on-prem Vault or cloud KMS only by explicit deployment profile. Encryption keys must never enter repo/issues/prompts/logs; test deliberate access denial and partial rotation. Source https://docs.aws.amazon.com/kms/latest/developerguide/kms-cryptography.html .
DATA-W3-02 Signing and Key Guardian: segregate signer roles by capability (dev, CI test, deployment, upgrade, treasury operations), threshold/multisig and timed high-value operations. Safe Smart Account and OpenZeppelin AccessManager/timelock can support approved EVM governance, but plugins/modules/guards create new security-critical authority paths requiring code and configuration review. HSM or institutional MPC/TSS can be evaluated for actual custody/scale; multisig, MPC and HSM solve different risks and are not automatic replacements for one another. Sources https://docs.safe.global/advanced/smart-account-overview , https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/access-control .
DATA-W3-03 Transaction Intent Firewall: bind signature request to approved user intent, chainId, verifying contract, function ABI and arguments, token, spender/allowance, amount/decimal, nonce, expiration, EIP-712 domain and message, EIP-1271 contract wallet validation if used and precise implementation of any optional account abstraction standards; show human-readable signed intent and flag unlimited approvals, dangerous delegatecall and unapproved session key use. Simulate transactions when provider supports deterministic context, fail closed on mismatch or uncertain critical permission; simulation is supplementary and cannot prove an unknown external contract is safe.
DATA-W3-04 Onchain Confidential Proof Adapter (EXPERIMENTAL): owner-approved zero knowledge proof when it actually reduces data disclosure and its circuits/public inputs remain auditable. Study Noir and field-appropriate modern proof systems, trusted setup/prover cost, verification keys, verifier upgrade policy, public input metadata leakage, circuit soundness and supported chain deployment. FHEVM/Zama enables encrypted computation in certain architectures but adds relayers, coprocessors, key/decryption ACLs and external assumptions; do not advertise all blockchain data as hidden. Source https://docs.zama.org/protocol/solidity-guides and https://github.com/zama-ai/fhevm .
DATA-W3-05 Smart Contract Economic Risk Sentinel: expand existing Smart Contract Guardian against custody drain, malicious permission, reentrancy, oracle attack, unexpected collateral/precision rounding, governance vote/invalidation, flash-loan-sensitive invariants, bridge/reorg/finality and proxy upgrades. Seed tests from 2025 OWASP taxonomy and any current more recent source after admission, rather than treating a Top 10 list as comprehensive. Source https://scs.owasp.org/sctop10/archive/2025/Top10%3A2025/ .
DATA-W3-06 Onchain/Offchain Confidential Reconciliation: avoid placing full private ledgers onto public chains; maintain private tenant-scoped accounting offchain, tokenized commitments/events only where justified. Tie each chain action to business event, chain/network, contract, finalized block hash/transaction/log index, indexer reorg/backfill handling, encrypted customer audit access and no double credit; reconcile against provider/ledger without leaking raw PII to indexer or logs.
DATA-W3-07 Governance and Incident Sentinel: independent threat reviews of admin, emergency pause, upgrade timelock, Safe module/guard, signer recovery and untrusted contract interaction; test the possibility that emergency guards block recovery or that governance delays prevent timely security response. Practice read-only monitoring, audit-ready incident drill and recovery under synthetic funds. No silent automatic privileged onchain operation.
DATA-W3-08 Crypto Agility Register: inventory cryptographic libraries, primitives and security dependencies, key usage, signing wallets and chain protocol constraints. NIST ML-KEM (FIPS 203), ML-DSA (FIPS 204) and SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) warrant long-term migration planning for appropriate offchain systems; they do not mean current EVM chain signatures can be arbitrarily replaced without protocol/account compatibility research. Source https://csrc.nist.gov/News/2024/postquantum-cryptography-fips-approved .
DATA-W3-09 Security Supply Chain and RPC Guardian: pin solc/Anchor/Rust/wallet libraries/prover versions and verified trusted binaries; separate RPC read from send rights, endpoint allowlist, RPC censorship/mismatch/reorg monitoring, provider failure/redundancy. Don't allow CI/forks to access production signer, paid RPC or real customer data. Reproducible pinned block forks are only simulations, not mainnet audit.
DATA-W3-10 Web3 Customer Risk UX: wallet connection, signing/approval/revoke visibility, wrong-chain warnings, phishing-resistant owner approved intent display, failure recovery, user security education and accessibility. Confirm no wallet login creates backend administrator privileges without verified nonce/domain/session binding; session invalidation and lost-wallet policy require explicit product/owner decision.

## 3. Conditional security controls and technology catalog
BASE for data-bearing Web3 apps: OWASP ASVS 5 authorization and multi-tenant access controls, PostgreSQL RLS or equivalent tenant isolation plus data-layer tests; envelope encryption in KMS/HSM if sensitive PII; strict secret scrubbing, audit logging with redaction, limited operator access, immutable high-value action receipts and actual restore drills. PostgreSQL RLS does not automatically cover table owner roles or TRUNCATE/REFERENCES; design least-privilege DB credentials and force correct policy behavior. Sources https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-rowsecurity.html and https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS/blob/master/5.0/en/0x17-V8-Authorization.md .
CRITICAL custody/high-value only: owner-approved threat model, qualified independent audit, multi-party/threshold authorization, key ceremony/recovery, contract state/role invariants, signed release artifact provenance, production deployment approval separated from code build and read-only operational monitoring.
EXPERIMENTAL privacy: ZK proofs, FHEVM, new MPC/threshold signer and privacy-preserving identity proofs start in SHADOW on synthetic-only fixtures. Accept only after threat proof, performance/gas/prover hardware budget, full lifecycle key management and a maintained upstream version.
Do not hardcode a specific vendor KMS, custody provider or privacy chain into core GEF; all are conditional adapters.

## 4. Tests and rejection gates
- Sensitive record mistakenly indexed on public chain or published as easily correlated PII hash must FAIL design admission.
- Cross-tenant API/row/object/field read/write and administrator impersonation FAIL; attempted data exfiltration via logs/caches/backups FAIL.
- Rotate/revoke offchain data key with recoverable existing ciphertext under planned version policy, restore encrypted backup, deny disabled key; verify all audit traces remain redacted.
- Stolen application credential cannot approve treasury upgrade, high-value transfer or privileged signer without separated authorization; simulate missing required multisig signers and recovery path.
- Replay/malicious network EIP-712 domain and nonce, unlimited token approval, fake token contract and phishing transaction intent produce explicit risk handling.
- Corrupted/mismatched circuit inputs or verifier key, ZK public metadata leakage and FHE encrypted data unauthorized decryption are tested where experimental profile approved.
- Expired upgrade/role/guard approval and unexpected arbitrary module cannot bypass governance. Test inability to recover from bad Safe guard or lost upgrade keys as high-assurance incident scenario.
- No security scanner/fuzz/property pass establishes “safe custody” without appropriate independent qualified review for actual risk classification.
- When toolchain, chain config, signer policy or financial contract scope drifts, invalidate relevant tests and previous proof receipts and execute required exact-head release sweep.

## 5. Legal/privacy interface
For Brazilian owners, the LGPD/ANPD security guidance and the current scope of Banco Central PSAV rules depend on whether the app is informational, noncustodial software, custodian, broker, intermediary, etc.; flag specialist review rather than making an automated legal conclusion. Current official anchors include ANPD guide https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia-orientativo-sobre-seguranca-da-informacao-para-agentes-de-tratamento-de-pequeno-porte and Banco Central Resolução BCB 520/2025 as amended by BCB 589/2026 https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/exibenormativo?numero=520&tipo=resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o+bcb .
PII subject rights are difficult to fulfill for public irreversible transactions; minimization/offchain encryption and a qualified privacy impact assessment precede any sensitive onchain schema.
