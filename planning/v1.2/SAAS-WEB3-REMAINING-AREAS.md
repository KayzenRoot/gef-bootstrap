# GEF V1.2 — SaaS Finance, Web3 Data Security and Coverage-Gap Review

STATUS: FOR FUTURE OWNER CLASSIFICATION, not a statement that any technology is installed, benchmarked or required by already accepted Scope. This review answers “which areas have we left out?” and protects startup velocity by separating a small universal foundation from costly specialized extensions.

## Newly recorded proposal map
SaaS Finance Factory: SaaS risk-aware interview, metered billing/entitlements, robust provider orchestration, financial ledger only when real balance/split needs justify it, reconciliation/fraud/chargebacks, unit economics and country/accounting handoff.
SaaS Tenant and Data Governance: identity, passkeys/MFA, cross-tenant isolation throughout API/DB/cache/object storage/vector/queues, privacy/data lifecycle, encryption/restore drills, incident response and enterprise evidence.
Web3 Data Security: no PII on irreversible public chain, encrypted offchain data vault, signer/custody authority and recovery, human-readable transaction intent, multisig/optional qualified threshold custody, EVM contract proof, ledger/indexer consistency and optional ZK/FHE research.
Existing approved and prior research layers remain authoritative: Marathon, Bug Hunter, Delta Assurance, Causal Repair, Autonomous Review, Engineering Intelligence, web/UI, Web3 Factory and Game Factory.

## Areas still worth exploring; each needs a bounded pilot and owner decision
GAP-S1 Fiscal/legal/financial product classifier: ordinary SaaS subscription vs money-moving marketplace vs provider/custody/exchange. Requirements differ; owner specifies operating country, funds custody, invoicing/tax and specialist compliance. Brazil official PSAV rules Resolução BCB 520/2025 with BCB 589/2026 amendments https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/exibenormativo?numero=520&tipo=resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o+bcb . ANPD LGPD startup checklist https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/materiais-educativos-e-publicacoes/guia-orientativo-sobre-seguranca-da-informacao-para-agentes-de-tratamento-de-pequeno-porte . Do not fabricate automated legal clearance.
GAP-S2 Real revenue operations beyond dashboards: durable invoice corrections, accountant export, country-specific fiscal documents, tax timing, revenue-vs-cash recognition, provider disputes, payout settlement calendar and evidence of reconciling external account statement to immutable internal ledger. Avoid interpreting MRR as bank balance.
GAP-S3 SaaS customer lifecycle and enterprise adoption: onboarding, workspace/team management, seats, support access/impersonation controls, SSO/SCIM, deletion/transfer/export and data residency. Offer only when target customer actually requires them.
GAP-S4 Product-led growth and onboarding experiments: trial-to-paid activation funnel, cancellation reason and ethical retention UX, transparent paywall and upgrade limits, privacy-respecting product analytics; no dark-pattern billing. Distinguish experimental conversion metrics from accepted engineering progress.
GAP-S5 Disaster/operational readiness: objective RPO/RTO, reversible deploy/canary, encrypted backup restore, incident exercises, cost alarms, SLO/error budgets and client-facing support communication; do not ship a platform that has only green CI and no proven recovery.
GAP-S6 Cross-border FX/stablecoin and treasury: decimal precision, FX rate source/finality/rounding, fees, settlement vs mark-to-market values, redemption/peg risk, asset custody and bank/provider insolvency assumptions. Only if product accepts cross-border or stablecoin funds. Independent qualified financial/legal review on appropriate risk.
GAP-W1 Cryptographic key-loss recovery and signer governance: threshold/multisig vs HSM/KMS/MPC tradeoff, separation of duties, emergency recovery, compromised signer rotation, Safe module/guard risks and mainnet high-value operational ceremony. No raw signing secrets in ChatGPT/GitHub.
GAP-W2 Privacy leak through chain metadata: commitments/hash correlation, address linking, event timing, external indexers/RPC providers, proof public inputs and blockchain immutability; prove metadata minimization before trying a fashionable privacy stack.
GAP-W3 Oracles/bridges/MEV/economic attacks: include only if protocol actually uses oracles, AMMs, flash-loan-sensitive pricing or cross-chain bridges. Model collateral/liquidity/finality and user loss limits. Scanner passing is not economic design certification.
GAP-W4 Formal methods and confidential compute ROI: optional zk circuit proving, FHEVM, symbolic/concolic smart contract verification, trusted execution where relevant, audited MPC. Need actual security property, public-input/threat assumptions, runtime/gas/prover/maintenance cost and independent assessment. Many apps can use offchain encryption instead.
GAP-W5 Crypto agility and long-term support: chain/compiler/wallet standard version matrix, upgrade/immutable incident options, NIST post-quantum standards for offchain systems and protocol compatibility. Never claim a generic post-quantum EVM signing switch exists.
GAP-X1 AI-native SaaS economics and privacy: per-tenant token/GPU limits, prompt/log privacy, model evaluation and abuse isolation; do not invoice from unreliable estimated token usage. Audit tool permissions and prevent prompt injection from external documents taking financial actions.
GAP-X2 Trusted payment/webhook and build supply chain: third-party payment page compliance, vendor key permissions, action SHA pins, artifact provenance, cross-tenant event routing, dependency and signer attestations with live plan/entitlement check.
GAP-X3 Hybrid game+Web3 economic conservation: marketplace/RMT, item issuance/burn and platform fees across offchain inventory and finalized chain event, no phantom asset availability, dispute/user recovery and tested asynchronous finality. Activate only on approved hybrid scope.

## Freeze guidance
A. Core v1.2 candidate after V1.1 acceptance: interview, provenance, exact status/forecast, Marathon Codex execution, safe tests and audit, conditional profile detection; one end-to-end reproducible web starter baseline. Do not delay entire core for an experimental ZK engine or enterprise ledger cluster.
B. Conditional SaaS pack: billing for subscription/usage pilot, tenant negative tests, provider-webhook sandbox and financial observability. Add Formance only for money-moving requirements, enterprise SSO only for enterprise pilot.
C. Conditional Web3 security pack: EVM local synthetic security pilot, offchain encrypted tenant records, safe contract roles/signature/invariants and separate human approval. Solana and privacy proof engines retain separate readiness and resource gates.
D. Optional higher-complexity packs: custodian/fintech payout ledgers, zero knowledge/FHE, multi-region failover, enterprise certification, institutional MPC, high-load MMO and Game+Web3 economy. These require evidence that the startup actually needs them before scope admission.
E. Each new idea must name specific user value or bug class, existing engine being extended, dependency/tool/license/cost, minimum working pilot, security/failure test, measurable benchmark and a stop/defer condition. Avoid expanding the v1.2 denominator by counting speculative research as product work.

## Pilot acceptance and operating cost
P1 basic paid SaaS: owner-defined country/provider, full subscription lifecycle, idempotent duplicate/reordered webhooks, access state, customer margin, data isolation, real preview; no real card collection in tests.
P2 metered AI SaaS: cost/consumption quotas, replayed billing events, hard limit/overage policy, exact tenant binding and comparably measured profitability.
P3 small noncustodial EVM SaaS: disposable contract/fork, typed signatures/nonce, offchain encrypted user data, safe simulated governance, indexed finality consistency and no onchain PII.
P4 money-moving/treasury: only if separately admitted, balanced ledger/reconciliation and high-assurance independent audit.
P5 optional proof privacy: ZK/FHE synthetic data, threat model and benchmark relative to simple offchain envelope encryption; FAIL if no measurable privacy need or untenable prover/operational cost.
For every pilot report accepted functional journeys and tests, owner signoffs, clock/CPU/CI cost, false positives, escape rate, revenue/operating cost where verifiable, recovery and review events. V1.1 ordinary web throughput is not a valid substitute for high-value smart-contract or money-moving work rates.

## Remaining owner questions at future WO-000
Payment geography and SaaS monetization model; whether the startup will ever hold third-party funds or merely charge access; first multi-tenant/enterprise customer requirements; first Web3 chain/custody/privacy property; first real-risk contract and qualified audit threshold; maximum paid CI/cloud budget; chosen small pilot for full release demonstration. An owner may defer decisions about out-of-scope products without stalling a well-defined core v1.2.
