# M52 S05 — Repair Suggestions
Status: FROZEN

**RSG52 Repair Suggestion Graph** maps proven finding codes to ranked repairs with risk, prerequisites, mutation scope, reversibility and verification. Suggestions are plans, not automatic writes. Auto-fix is allowed only for separately policy-approved low-risk idempotent repairs with preview and receipt.

Acceptance: finding-evidence binding, risk/reversibility, preview-first, no unsafe auto-fix, post-repair verification.
