# M45 S03 — GEF Gains
Status: FROZEN

**GAM45 Gain Attribution Matrix** separates observed deltas into context reuse, test-impact selection, parallelism, reduced correction churn, retrieval efficiency and UNKNOWN. Attribution is evidence-based and may remain partial.

Reported gains include numerator/denominator and baseline id. Negative gains are first-class results. No marketing-style aggregate is allowed when dimensions conflict.

**AQG45 Assurance Quality Guard** requires correctness/security evidence alongside efficiency claims.

Acceptance: transparent formulas, negative/unknown support, evidence references, no double-counting, assurance guard.
