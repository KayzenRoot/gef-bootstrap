# M45 S02 — Before & After
Status: FROZEN

**CAC45 Comparable Cohort Gate** allows before/after comparison only when workload, assurance profile and material environment dimensions are compatible or explicitly normalized. Otherwise result is `NOT_COMPARABLE`.

**BCI45 Benchmark Confidence Interval** reports sample count, central tendency, dispersion and uncertainty rather than a naked percentage. Small samples are labeled insufficient.

No benchmark may trade correctness/security for speed without separately reporting the assurance change.

Acceptance: cohort compatibility, insufficient-sample handling, uncertainty reporting, assurance-equivalence requirement.
