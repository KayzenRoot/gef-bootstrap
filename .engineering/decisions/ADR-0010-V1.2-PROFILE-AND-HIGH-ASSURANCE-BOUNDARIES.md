## Context

The Phase A+B audit found substantial existing capability in M14-M18, M20-M28, M31, M43, M45 and M63, plus conditional application and domain proposals. A single mixed denominator would turn optional profile failures into universal blockers and obscure the accepted V1.1.2 baseline. Existing evidence also does not make an owner-operated semantic audit independent.

## Decisions formalized for proposal

1. V1.2 release claims use a finite universal-core denominator plus separate denominators for each selected conditional profile.
2. The first end-to-end pilot is full-stack multi-tenant SaaS. It covers the selected web/API, data, identity/authorization, tenant-isolation, subscription/entitlement, security, recovery, observability and release-evidence surfaces. It excludes real-money movement, custody, escrow, payouts and live financial transactions.
3. The EVM reference uses Solidity, Foundry and Anvil and remains conditional. No production key custody, real-fund signing, asset movement or mainnet authority is implied.
4. Godot 4 is the first conditional game reference. Deterministic/headless proof is preferred where supported; platform targets are profile-declared and tested.
5. Initial paid-tool and expanded-CI budget is US$ 0. A later paid tool, CI expansion or service needs a separate owner-approved, evidence-backed decision before use.
6. HIGH_ASSURANCE includes financial value movement; custody or escrow; blockchain transaction signing; smart contracts controlling real value; privileged authentication/authorization; irreversible migrations; and destructive/irreversible privileged operations. Before production, require an independent specialist review bound to the exact candidate and profile. If unavailable, stale, failed or unresolved, remain BLOCKED and do not claim production readiness.
7. Owner-operated ChatGPT semantic audit remains NOT_INDEPENDENT. It is not the specialist review and cannot satisfy that independent gate.

## Consequences and non-decisions

The V1.1.2 production acceptance record and denominator stay unchanged. Conditional profiles do not enter the universal denominator unless independently selected under their acceptance contracts. OD-01 selects SaaS as the first pilot for a separate profile-level proof; it does not make financial movement or custody part of the pilot. This ADR does not select payment providers, cloud vendors, paid tools, legal/custody policy, a production chain, or a game distribution service.

This is a Phase D proposal only. It authorizes no product code, tests, CI, migration, dependencies, checkpoint promotion, merge, release, publication, deployment or WO-001 execution.
