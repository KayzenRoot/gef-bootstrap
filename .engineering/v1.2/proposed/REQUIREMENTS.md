# Proposed V1.2 Requirements Delta

**State:** `PROPOSED / NOT_CANONICAL`

## R-V12-001 Guided discovery
The system shall capture unresolved owner/product decisions as typed questions, preserve answered/unanswered state and feed approved answers into existing planning/decision/scope authorities.

## R-V12-002 No parallel planning authority
Discovery and planning UX shall not create a second canonical source. Canonical planning remains bound to current Source Pack, decision and scope/DoD authorities.

## R-V12-003 Marathon orchestration
Long-running work shall compile into resumable, evidence-bound execution waves using current context/task/execution/checkpoint/resume authorities.

## R-V12-004 Safe parallelism
Parallel work shall be admitted only when dependency, guardrail and validation closures prove it safe. Unknown dependency state widens or serializes execution.

## R-V12-005 Bug Hunter
The assurance layer shall map requirements/invariants to proof obligations and may invoke profile-appropriate contract/property/mutation adapters without creating a parallel proof store.

## R-V12-006 Delta Assurance
Selective validation and proof reuse shall remain fail-closed, lineage-aware and incapable of suppressing the required final exact-head sweep.

## R-V12-007 Causal repair
Automated repair shall identify and record a causal failure fingerprint, bounded proposed correction and post-correction evidence. Blind repeated patching is not accepted evidence.

## R-V12-008 Flake control
Repeated inconsistent failures shall be classified and recorded separately from deterministic product defects. Flake handling may not convert an unknown/failing release gate to green without evidence.

## R-V12-009 Review truth
Owner exact-head audit shall be recorded as NOT_INDEPENDENT. Reviewer packets may automate evidence preparation but shall not claim independent assurance from the same implementation/governance loop.

## R-V12-010 Engineering Intelligence
Performance/cost/quality comparisons shall use matched populations and explicit MEASURED/ESTIMATED/UNAVAILABLE states. Incomparable populations must produce INCOMPARABLE, not an improvement claim.

## R-V12-011 Status composition
The product shall expose one compact status projection derived from current response/progress/estimation/project-status authorities, including next legal action and changed-since-last-state evidence.

## R-V12-012 Profile routing
Project/domain profile selection shall be owner-confirmed when material. Detection may recommend but shall not silently select high-risk domain behavior.

## R-V12-013 Reversible installation
Tool/profile installation plans shall be scoped, reproducible and reversible. Optional tools shall not become mandatory core dependencies without explicit admission.

## R-V12-014 Visual experience
UI-bearing targets shall support a governed visual acceptance contract. Browser/design tooling is activated only for applicable profiles.

## R-V12-015 Reference profile
WEB_APP_API shall prove the complete core journey in a controlled reproducible target before core V1.2 production acceptance.

## R-V12-016 Conditional domain packs
Non-reference D-series packs shall not enter the core release denominator without explicit owner admission.

## R-V12-017 Cost posture
The default validation/tool posture shall be LOCAL_FREE_FIRST. Paid services require explicit owner approval.

## R-V12-018 Specialist review
High-risk production readiness shall require qualified specialist review for the owner-approved trigger classes.

## R-V12-019 Release integrity
V1.2 distribution shall preserve or strengthen current immutable artifact, provenance, signature, recovery and public retrieval verification.

## R-V12-020 No regression
V1.2 shall not weaken V1.1.2 required status checks, fail-closed semantics, accepted security posture or exact-head release assurance.
