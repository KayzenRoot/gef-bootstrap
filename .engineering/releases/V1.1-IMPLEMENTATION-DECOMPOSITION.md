## 4. Readiness gates for successor Work Orders

A successor WO may be admitted only when:

1. its contract authority in §2 exists and is audited;
2. `ADR-0003-D3` has completed objective audit and governed checkpoint promotion under `D-0042`;
3. its base branch descends from `release/1.1` at or after the WO-001 merge and promotion;
4. it declares SCOPE, OUT OF SCOPE, acceptance criteria, evidence bundle and STOP CONDITION;
5. its verification path names concrete test-matrix case IDs;
6. it inherits the `ADR-0003-D3` execution bounds verbatim;
7. it does not modify V1.0.0 acceptance history, `CHECKPOINT.*` production state or the `v1.0.0` tag.

## 5. Out of scope for WO-001 (explicit)

Per the Work Order OUT OF SCOPE: no broad implementation of WO-002..WO-010, no package/release publication, no merge to `main`, no movement of `v1.0.0`, no breaking of accepted V1 contracts without a governed compatibility decision, no unrelated refactors or cosmetic cleanup.

WO-001 produces **specification and governed decision artifacts plus their validation tests** only.
